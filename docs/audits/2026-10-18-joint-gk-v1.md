## Model contract

For a represented competitive GK set, retain B's exclusive start marginals. For
a lone viable keeper, or a verified incomplete club set, retain the original
MM probability **before available-roster projection**, with remaining mass
assigned to the unlisted group. The downstream B calibrator cannot undo a
certainty already created upstream by excluding unlisted competitors. No new
probability floor, hand-selected player coefficient, or forced 90-minute player
forecast is introduced.

Conditional starter duration is an empirical posterior PMF on 1..90, using only
GK starts known strictly before the input cutoff. The existing strength-five
shrinkage and 180-day half-life are reused; personal duration history remains
club-specific. Bench/cameo observations do not become starter-duration samples.
The league prior handles an inexperienced keeper such as Penders.

Select one GK starter. Sample his duration from that PMF; if shorter than90,
select an eligible reserve and give that reserve exactly the remaining interval.
Injured/unavailable players receive neither start nor reserve weight. An explicit
unlisted pool covers missing reserves. The pool's event opportunity uses the
existing team GK baseline, without attributing its points to a guessed player.

For keeper j, the unconditional minute PMF is the mixture of his starter PMF and
all reserve intervals he can receive when another keeper starts. MM xM,
P(60+), P(90), cameo probability and conditional cameo mean are computed from
that exact PMF. PM samples the same process. Every team/fixture has exactly90
analytic and realized GK minutes with no overlap. An existing dependent-rounding
primitive samples ten outfield starters without changing their B marginals;
together with the categorical GK this gives exactly11 starters per team.
This is a starting-XI/GK coverage guarantee, not a new full outfield substitution
model or a production mathematical promotion.
