# Player card: non-PL matches and absence context

Add an independent, checksum-verified public match-context dataset for the current season's completed PL, Champions League, Europa League, Conference League/qualification, FA Cup and EFL Cup fixtures involving current PL clubs. Select inventory by exact competition and season, completed status and UTC cutoff; map players by exact Opta/FPL stable code, never names. Match PL fixtures by exact home/away FPL team IDs plus kickoff UTC. Reject conflicting identities and duplicate player/match rows. Missing inventories/details/identities remain explicit gaps. Model files, original collection, forecasts and manager state are untouched.

The existing player modal, profile and embedded Transfers renderer now show non-PL opponent/date/result, minutes, goals, assists, xG/xA, shots and participation/substitution context, plus recorded seven-day workload. Non-PL matches never award displayed FPL points or silently change existing xP. Official FPL PL history remains its own source. Zero-minute PL rows show source-linked reasons where observed, and unknown otherwise. Reported injuries/suspensions and substitution reasons are attributed to the provider. Unknown reasons do not become rest/rotation. Confirmed causal claims require curated evidence with `cause_confirmed: true`.
