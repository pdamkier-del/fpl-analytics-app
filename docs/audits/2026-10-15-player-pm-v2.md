## Sources and what can be identified

The full Core and raw-source inventory from the preceding PM audit is reused, not replaced. `data_audit.json` gives non-null counts and source coverage. Core contains final FPL outcomes, xG/xA, minutes, broad FPL positions, registrations and canonical identities across four seasons. Early 2022/23 expected-stat placeholders are excluded. Historical availability uses the inherited kickoff+3/4h proxies: no claim of actual publication-time certification. Team IDs are keyed by season.

Raw 2024 shotmaps contain verified shots and non-penalty labels but disagree with Core on some xG values. Raw performance ledgers already provide chance-creation/SOT features to the original allocator. They are not silently filled or spliced into a new multi-season training set. No complete missed-shot creator/receiver network or time-valid 2024/25–2025/26 set-piece order exists. Detailed tactical role changes are not observable from broad FPL positions alone. Those quantities remain Not Available rather than fabricated pair effects or role labels. Defensive/raw BPS profiles report only actually non-null histories and their dispersion. Scoring retains the original defensive/BPS component models; raw total BPS is never mistaken for background BPS.

## A. Player profiles

For a past match j, exposure is e_ij = minutes_ij/90 × d_j, where d_j is an opponent/venue factor calculated from observations available **before that past kickoff**. It is not estimated using the target game's xG. At the forecast cutoff c, calendar weights are w_j = 2^(-(c-known_j)/half_life).

Chance production and creation use conditional empirical-Bayes Gamma/quasi-Poisson profiles:

    q_i | history ~ Gamma(sum(w*xG) + tau*q_prior,
                          sum(w*e) + tau)

The mean is shape/rate, with conditional SD sqrt(shape)/rate and Gamma quantiles. xG is fractional, so these are quasi-likelihood/EB uncertainty summaries, not an exact shot-level Bayesian posterior. Simple profiles shrink toward the historical FPL-position mean. Hierarchical profiles first shrink current-season team/position production toward that mean, then the player toward the team/position prior. Strengths 5/20/80 90-minute exposures and calendar half-lives 180/365 days are predeclared development choices. The team-prior strength is four times the player strength. No player-specific tuning occurs.

Individual finishing uses (weighted_goals+20)/(weighted_xG+20), the fixed effective-calibration prior from earlier research. It is not claimed to isolate non-penalty skill. Assist conversion is estimated with uncertainty and reported, not automatically interpreted as creator talent or multiplied into assists independently of goals. Historical xA drives creator weights. Club moves retain opponent-neutralized player evidence but change the current team/position prior. A new FPL position uses the matching-role evidence or its prior, rather than relabeling old events. Transition groups use only preceding known observations, scoped by season for club identities.

## B. Lineup-dependent team process

For each sampled MM lineup and each of 90 minute bins, I_i(t) indicates whether player i is on the pitch. Let q_i and a_i be profile xG90 and xA90, and d the target opponent/venue multiplier:

    Q(t) = d * sum_i I_i(t) q_i
    A(t) = d * sum_i I_i(t) a_i
    X(t) = [(1-h) Q(t) + h lambda_original] * [A(t)/A_anchor]^beta

Pure player models use h=0 and beta=0. Hybrid candidates test h in {0.25,0.5,0.75} and beta in {0,0.25}. They are convex blends, not sums of full player and team forecasts. The creative elasticity is a regularized aggregate interaction, not learned specific player-pair chemistry. The historical team xA anchor and opponent factor are available before cutoff.

Let P be the original expected penalty-goal mass. Ordinary goal intensity is:

    L(t) = max(X(t)-P,0) * sum_i(I_i q_i theta_i)/sum_i(I_i q_i)

Individual ordinary intensities are L(t) times normalized I_i q_i theta_i. They sum exactly to L(t); finishing is applied once, not twice. The original penalty process/takers/conversion remain separate and unchanged. The all-xG versus non-penalty allocation limitation remains explicit. No active scorer yields no ordinary goal.

The original simulator is reused. A serial offline context collects its sampled minutes, replaces only the two ordinary Poisson means and draws ordinary goal times from the corresponding piecewise intensity CDF. Scorer/assist selection, opponent goals-conceded/clean sheets, penalties, discipline, defensive contributions, BPS and FPL scoring retain original functions. Assists remain attached to ordinary goal events with no self-assist. In particular, no new independent assist Poisson process is introduced. The original independent-XI physics is retained in the controlled comparison, not certified as a legal joint lineup model.

This is a genuinely player-dependent team forecast: removing a productive player changes Q(t), and substitutions change both total intensity and event timing. A regression explicitly distinguishes this from simply changing shares of fixed team lambda. The hybrid can damp noisy profiles, particularly for new arrivals and low-history players.

## Walk-forward protocol

Development: 2024/25 GW6–21. Select simple and hierarchical regularization by mean team-xG quasi-Poisson deviance from analytic expected-MM exposures; select hybrid h/beta on the same earlier period. Persist selection before reading later target forecast records. No realized target lineup, minutes, xG or goals enters predictions. Later periods: 2024/25 GW22–38 and 2025/26 GW6–38. These seasons were reused in prior research and are not untouched whole-model holdouts.

Full replay uses the same original captured inputs, deadlines and 400-draw seeds. Compare original, simple, hierarchical and hybrid under **locked MM first**. Separately compare unchanged PM against the development-selected player candidate under the same experimental start/survival MM. The two locked-MM original references must reproduce exactly. Every row's ten point components must sum to xP. Four deterministic shards per season/MM arm preserve seeds and allow resumable per-fixture signature caches. Incomplete or duplicate shard matrices fail aggregation.

Reports include team xG/goals MAE, RMSE, bias and quasi-Poisson deviance; individual goal/assist proper scores; xP/bonus/BPS and calibration; paired fixture-bootstrap intervals; limited-history, starter/rotation/reserve and prior club/role-transition groups. Bootstrap sampling uncertainty, conditional profile uncertainty and Monte Carlo noise are different and will not be conflated. A complete historical six-GW TS replay is still blocked by missing inputs; no candidate TS total will be invented.

## References and limitations

Methodological context: [A Bayesian approach for determining player abilities in football](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00001), [Bayes-xG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13707), [StatsBomb: Upgrading Expected Goals](https://blogarchive.statsbomb.com/articles/soccer/upgrading-expected-goals/). These motivate hypotheses, not evidence that this candidate improves our application. We do not have the shot-context/tracking fields needed to reproduce those richer models.

No production promotion without independent validation. The new code is isolated in `experimental_player_pm.py`, `research_player_pm_v2.py` and its research workflow. Private manager state is neither needed nor used.

### Usikkerhed

Profiler gemmer middel, SD, Gamma-parametre, betingede 95 %-intervaller og eksponering. Aktuelle verificerede actionprofiler og soft-role-fordelinger er et separat supplement, ikke historiske inputs uden tilsvarende dækning.

Skeln mellem betinget EB-profilusikkerhed, lineup/kamp-variation og Monte Carlo-støj samt historisk samplingusikkerhed. Fokus bruger også 1.000 uafhængige profildraws ved fast forventet MM-eksponering. Hybridens betingede målmiddel-intervaller er cirka Chelsea [1,1432; 1,8411], City [2,0033; 3,0754], Arsenal [1,3185; 2,0098]. Det er ikke fulde Bayes-intervaller eller faktiske scoreintervaller: fælles prior-/rolle-/holdparametre og MM-usikkerhed er ikke fuldt integreret. Fast forventet eksponering er heller ikke samme funktional som middel over samplede lineups.

### Lineup-variation og no-goal-masse

## Endelig anbefaling og resterende arbejde

**Promovér ikke PM v2 i den nuværende form.** Arkitekturen fungerer som en egentlig spillermodel: hvem der spiller og bliver skiftet ind/ud påvirker både holdtotal, scorerfordeling, assistmuligheder og hændelsestid. Men den har endnu ikke dokumenteret en robust samlet præcisionsgevinst.

Prioritér videreudvikling således:

1. Test den allerede eksisterende lovlige joint-XI-kandidat som fælles fysik under både original PM og spiller-PM. Vurdér derefter lineup-varians og CS-kalibrering med korrekt opstillingsstøtte. Den nuværende Shapley-opdeling beviser ikke, hvor stor en forbedring dette vil give.
2. Bevar det hierarkiske xA-/assistspor som forskningskandidat: event-log-loss forbedres i begge sæsoner. Validér FPL-assistregler og bonus samlet.
3. Forbedr priorer/usikkerhed for reserver, nye spillere og klubskifte; udvid ikke ukritisk individualisering ved sparsom historik.
4. Afstem flerårs npxG, skaber/receiver-events og tidsmærkede taktiske roller før mere komplekse personlige samspilsparametre estimeres.
5. Præregistrér acceptmål for forventede point, kalibrering og subgroup-robusthed på en **ny uafhængig periode**. Gentagne testperioder og Monte Carlo-støj tillader ikke produktionsgodkendelse her.

Historiske faktiske publiceringstider, kildecertificering og tidligere BPS/MM-mangler er fortsat blokeringer. Historiske seks-GW-inputs dækker kun 33/183 tidligere auditerede origin-target-celler; der er derfor ingen kandidat-TS-total. Det låste benchmark **2.125 point, 44 transfers, 28 hitpoint** er bevaret. Ingen private managerdata er anvendt eller publiceret.

Produktions-MM, original PM/vFinal, TS, chips og deres koefficienter er uændrede. Ingen merge eller app-deploy. **locked_model_active=false, promotion_allowed=false.** Fase A/B er leveret som reproducerbar forskning med reelle kandidater og fulde historiske tests; den er ikke præsenteret som en certificeret erstatningsmodel.
