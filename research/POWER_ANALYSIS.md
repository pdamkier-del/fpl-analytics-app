# Initial power / minimum-detectable-effect plan

No measured efficacy power is claimed. The only genuine recent public evidence spans three seen GWs, and the prospective substitution trial has no completed new GW in its latest observed receipt. Player counts or400simulationdraws do not create independent GW replicates.

## Paired planning unit

For a new prediction study define d_g as the candidate-minus-baseline mean proper score for a whole GW on the same eligible rows. Define practical delta in native units before seeing test means. A rough two-sided paired-normal planning formula is:

n_GW ≈ ((z_(1−alpha/2)+z_(power)) × sigma_GW / delta)^2.

At unadjusted alpha=.05 and80%target power the coefficient is about7.85. These are hypothetical variance scenarios, not estimates from our data:

| --- | --- | --- |

|0.05points|0.01points|197|
|0.02points|0.02points|8 before any minimum-review rule|

A practical12-GW review floor is not proof of power.197GWs would exceed a season; report infeasibility rather than shrink the target after seeing results. Adjustment for multiple primary tests increases requirements. These numbers are illustrative algebra, not candidate hyperparameters or a replacement for any prior registration.

## Initial estimation plan

- Before candidate evaluation, use eligible historical paired GW residual blocks only for exploratory sigma ranges, label reused data and check model/rule/source comparability. Three recent GWs cannot estimate a stable sigma reliably.
- If needed, prespecify a blinded variance re-estimation after an initial number of new blocks, without examining the signed candidate mean or changing endpoints. Fix the maximum sample and rule before any test outcomes are unblinded.
- Show sensitivity to sigma, clinically/practically relevant delta, serial/block dependence and population weighting. Use GW block resampling for prediction; block length must cover overlap/serial dependence. Do not assume n_match or n_player independent replicates.
- Use small-sample t/block methods and state their limitations. A nonsignificant result is not noninferiority or equivalence; those need prespecified margins and suitable intervals.
- For rare penalty saves, red cards, specific injuries and chips, count eligible events/decision episodes and feasibility first. Thousands of inactive player rows do not power a rare-event effect.

## TS-specific feasibility

Exact-small correctness is deterministic finite-domain verification, so statistical power is not applicable. Stateful season/chip gain has few independent initial-manager/season trajectories and overlapping decisions. Six-GW origins require nonoverlap or≥6GWblocks;12overlappingorigins may be only2informativeblocks. Individual counterfactual outcomes are model- and state-dependent. Do not claim population-wide TS gain from one manager or a single chosen initialization.
