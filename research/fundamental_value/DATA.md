# Data audit

- Repository base (main) at `d4d6e8046ad5d965cf41f5a8fce2b32a49fca16c`. The [main archive handoff](../../integration/research-data/C1_DATA_AVAILABILITY.md) documents multiple genuine original versions from one GW origin. [PR56's research branch](https://github.com/pdamkier-del/fpl-analytics-app/tree/e15e56775cdcdaa924f75ee1799173b7eace3fb8/research/ts_realpool) documents one original full pool and 12 generated states. This coverage cannot supply cross-season frozen MM/PM forecasts for a TS v3 comparison. Only public research inputs were used.
- Historical [Vaastav snapshot](https://github.com/vaastav/Fantasy-Premier-League/tree/7ca01d3821a68ced454cd1f2195be40ed697629b/data) covers 2022–23 through 2024–25 in this study. See [counts and SHA-256](results/data_audit.json). The source's xP column was excluded. Rows for auxiliary positions were filtered. Player/fixture keys are unique after filtering and fixture IDs are matched. Player-week aggregation sums outcomes for double gameweeks and uses the last fixture record's market fields.
- At an evaluation origin, the primary model uses records from at least two GWs earlier, with fixture kickoffs more than five days before the target round's first kickoff. This protects against adjacent or out-of-order fixture leakage under ordinary match and collection timing. The archive does not provide immutable prices, ownership or transfer-flow snapshot timestamps. Historical corrections and exact execution availability remain unverified. The source's 2022–23 GW7 lacks match rows and the common start was moved to GW8.
- Without fixture rows, scoring treats players as absent. The price panel requires records at both outcome endpoints. This excludes some blanks, exits and missing records, potentially biasing associations. Players without a usable recent snapshot are outside the candidate pool. The replay carries forward previously known club and price for held players absent from a snapshot. The final fixture calendar is used for the explicitly retrospective market sensitivity. The primary estimates omit upcoming schedule information.
- There is no event-vintage medical or tactical-role data in these files. Return and starter indicators are proxies. The analysis uses absolute lagged selection counts within origin rather than estimated percentages of active managers.
