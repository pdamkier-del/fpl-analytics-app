## Gaps that must remain explicit

Verified exact substitution times/pairs/causes, injury diagnosis and comeback evidence, complete historically available future Europe/cup schedules, missing ordinary save subtypes, background/event BPS joint draws, per-fixture DGW minute/point laws where only aggregates were stored, complete historical six-GW vintages and private nominated/executed decisions. Unknown fields are not zero. Current FPL status, prices or squad lists cannot be used as old features; current stable codes may verify outcome identity without replacing old forecast metadata.

Every new dataset item needs license/use basis, units, source identifiers, event/publication/ingestion timestamps, missingness, revisions and scope of disclosure. MIT code licensing does not grant redistribution rights over underlying provider sports data. Commercial raw forecasts or private manager files never enter this public catalog.
