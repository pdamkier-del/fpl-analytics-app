## Distinguish data from inference

- FPL live saves is an observed count. Forecast save-point means do not identify its count distribution.
- Some targets ARE identified: outfield fixture P(DCaward)=E[DCpoints]/2, qualified-player P(CS)=E[CSpoints]/position_award for nonzero positional awards, and goal/assist count means from their linear point multipliers. This does not identify teamCS, full count laws, or joint BPS dependence.
- Current FPL clean-sheet fields are direct outcome labels; historical PR32 clean sheets were rule-derived and must keep that label.
- PR32 contains historical marginal p_bps/bg_rate/bg_sd; current public player scenarios do not contain the corresponding full event/background trace. Do not generalize either cohort to the other.
- Missing historical penalty-goal or ordinary big-chance-save labels remain unknown. No xGOTthreshold or current webpage is substituted as a historical feature.
- Current official articles are rule evidence observed today, not proof of their original historic delivery time. All old before-deadline forecast issues stay unchanged.
- Source comparisons and known identities are deterministic contract checks; no gain, confidence interval or independent validation is claimed.
