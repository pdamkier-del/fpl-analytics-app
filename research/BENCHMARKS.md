## Prediction references

1. Reuse the completed official FPL ep_next comparison from PR41; do not rerun it as a new discovery. Continue only with a same-origin ep_next from a genuinely archived future catalogue. Current ep_next cannot backfill a missing old value.
2. Simple MM/PM references must use prior-only labels, preserve target definitions and honestly report inability to predict an event. A league-rate, last-match/persistence or shrunk prior is informative but beating it does not establish state-of-the-art performance.
3. FPL Review: verify the model/product, authorized export/API, numerical field definitions, original timestamps, player/fixture mapping and right to store/analyse/share derived results. Subscription access is not automatically scraping or data redistribution permission.
4. FPL Form: its export page distinguishes unconditional xP and appearance-conditional values; personal export and derived-analysis credit do not permit republishing raw files. No file is incorporated now.
5. LazyFPL: dated newsletters and advice exist, but a legally usable stable-ID numeric player-xP export was not verified. Newsletters, captain choices or graphics cannot be converted into invented player probabilities/point means.
6. Open-source model classes such as OpenFPL can later be compared on the same data and forecasts; published other-season scores cannot be pasted next to our three-GW scores as a head-to-head result. Check code and data rights separately.

## TS references

Separate legality/scoring correctness, heuristic search gap, forecast expected utility and realized policy quality. Exact-small is the first new implementable study; its domain is finite and explicit. Keep-squad and minimal-necessary-transfer policies share the original initial state, price/sale-price/FT/chip information and model vintage. For realized policies, each arm evolves its own feasible state; do not give every policy the hindsight-winning squad each week.

Horizon length/discounting, hit-buffer, risk-penalty and roll-FT policies are proposed sensitivity families only. No tuning now. Complete original horizons and a reserved chronological test trajectory are prerequisites. Chip horizons must include their stopping/availability rules; chipsOFF results cannot validate any chip strategy.

## Reporting guardrails

Report matched coverage and omissions, all ex-ante groups, raw effect units and appropriate paired intervals. Missing external distributions mean no external CRPS/haul-Brier score. Reject later revisions as historical forecasts unless the original vintage is independently evidenced. Raw paid data and private trajectories remain private; disclose only what rights and the privacy protocol permit.
