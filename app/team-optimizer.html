How to read the score
Raw xPts gain is the expected gain after transfer hits. Decision gain also subtracts the uncertainty buffer for each transfer. That buffer prevents small model differences from causing unnecessary weekly churn.
Rolling plan may explicitly say “roll” for a GW and bank the FT for later. Future weeks currently allow at most one planned transfer per GW after the current GW to keep the search fast and stable.
Bench: normal-GW bench xPts are not in the objective, so a cheap burner can be preferred when it funds a better XI.
