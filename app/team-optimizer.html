Why a burner can be optimal
Primary objective
Maximise XI xPts + captain bonus − transfer hits. Bench xPts are not added to the normal-GW objective.
Bench role
The bench only has to keep the 15-man squad legal. Cheap players are intentionally included in the candidate pool so cash can move into the XI.
Constraints
Same-position transfers, current-price budget, maximum 3 players per club, and legal FPL formations every GW.

Method & limitations
Budget: until the app has your exact purchase/selling prices, transfer affordability uses the players' current listed prices plus your saved bank. Search: incoming candidates are pruned to high-xPts, high-value and cheap options, then a beam search keeps both strong immediate states and cash-releasing states so two-step burner + upgrade plans are not discarded. Not included yet: Wildcard/Free Hit full rebuilds, Bench Boost objective and autosub probability.
