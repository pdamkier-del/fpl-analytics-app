How the plan works
Rolling mode: the model can roll a transfer and make it in a later GW if that gives more total xPts. Free transfers roll forward up to 5.
Hits: -4 is charged only when the plan uses more transfers than are available in that GW.
Bench: bench xPts do not count in the normal-GW objective, so cheap burners are allowed to release budget into the XI.
