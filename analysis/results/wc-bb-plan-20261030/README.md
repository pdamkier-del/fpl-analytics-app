# Conditional Bench Boost in the B Wildcard horizon

The existing WC squad and original subsequent transfer path are retained. After each round's proposed transfers, the existing BB estimator evaluates the actual future squad against the ordinary lineup. In the B runtime it uses the same aligned PM points/minutes draws and subtracts ordinary autosub points. No independent-appearance approximation, raw-bench double counting, parameter refit or additional transfer search is introduced.

A positive surplus above the locked BB threshold is required. Among eligible future dates in each chip period, choose the highest TS-weighted surplus; ties prefer earlier dates. At most one BB per period, never the current WC deadline, no reuse of a spent right, and no collision with an explicitly reserved other chip. A period reset may allow a BB in each of the two halves. Low/negative value leaves BB unused. Chosen BB uplifts enter the round score, utility, six-GW total and weighted objective; without-BB scores/totals remain available for comparison.

Scope: conditional BB timing on a fixed WC/transfer path. The WC roster and transfers are NOT cooptimized for BB. This does not add a complete future FH/TC calendar. Current WC arbitration and normal/production plans remain unchanged; no claim of historical predictive improvement or a global optimal chip policy.

Frontend shows BB on its planned GW, incremental value, updated total and an optional table of every future BB opportunity with bench, threshold and reason. Old cached paths offer capability-checked WC+BB recomputation. WC gain is explicitly labelled before future BB, since adding BB value to a WC-vs-normal chip comparison without evaluating BB on both sides would be invalid.
